## Editorial and accuracy notes

- No Friday events or already-closed Saturday daytime events were carried over. Saturday daytime museum, HallZOOween, Burger Farm, native plant sale, and matinee performances were removed. Saturday Renaissance Festival and Sauerkraut Festival were omitted because travel would consume most of the time remaining.
- Ongoing entries are named temporary exhibitions or dated seasonal programs, not generic zoo, park or museum attraction filler.
- BLINK has its overall two nightly cards plus three distinct programmed destinations: Asianati Night Market, Polka-DOTober and the one-night Redtree Speakeasy. No individual projections, duplicate showtimes or animal enrichment sessions were split into padding cards.
- Price and age restrictions remain Check source when not published. Ticket inventory and sellout status can change. Listed prices are published face values/ranges, not checkout-total quotes.
- Drive estimates are approximate from central Cincinnati, without live traffic. No 91–120 minute road trips were needed. BLINK crowds/road closures can substantially affect downtown travel.
- Kelsey Cook’s 6:30 PM show is imminent; the same card also contains the 9:15 PM show. If publication happens after the early performance, remove its elapsed listing and keep the late set. Other Saturday start-only events need another timing check if publication is delayed into the evening.
- Here Come the Mummies: official promoter lists 7 PM, while some secondary sources describe that as doors and 8 PM as the set. The card explicitly labels 7 PM as the listed event time and asks readers to check the set time.
- Polka-DOTober’s official umbrella schedule says 7–11 PM. A separate STINKSTOCK landing page had a 7–10 PM headline; the card describes the umbrella event and does not claim STINKSTOCK itself ends at 11.
- Last-successful-edition metadata remains October 3–4 because this worker did not publish. Update it only after actual publication is confirmed.
